XYZ Warehousing Hub

A logistics and warehousing park positioned on Coimbatore's transport belt, built for e-commerce and manufacturing demand.

About This Project

As e-commerce and contract manufacturing scaled up across Coimbatore, warehousing supply didn't keep pace — especially units built to modern specifications with genuine highway access. XYZ Warehousing Hub was developed to serve exactly that demand: 50+ industrial units positioned directly on the city's logistics belt.

Nexus Capital structured the investment and helped secure early anchor tenants from the e-commerce and third-party logistics sectors. Within its first year of operation, the park reached 95% occupancy — a track record that continues to draw investor interest in the remaining units.

Project Highlights

  • 95% occupancy achieved within the first year
  • 50+ industrial & warehousing units
  • Direct access to major highways and transport hubs
  • High-clearance units suited to modern racking systems
  • 24/7 security and dedicated loading bays
  • Flexible unit sizes for e-commerce & manufacturing tenants
