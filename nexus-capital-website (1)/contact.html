FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I start investing with Nexus Capital?

Simply reach out via our contact form, phone, or WhatsApp. Our advisory team will schedule a consultation to understand your goals and recommend suitable opportunities.

What is the minimum investment amount?

Minimum investment varies by project and sector. Our advisory team will share specific figures for opportunities that match your budget during your consultation.

Do you work with first-time investors?

Yes. We guide both first-time and experienced investors, offering clear, transparent explanations at every step of the process.

Which areas of Coimbatore do you focus on?

We cover residential, commercial, industrial, and mixed-use opportunities across Coimbatore's key growth corridors, including the IT corridor and logistics belt.

Is there ongoing support after I invest?

Absolutely. Our portfolio management service continues to track your investment's performance and keeps you informed with regular market insights.
